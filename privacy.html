Cookies and analytics

Synky Software Ltd (UK) operates this site. Under UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), we ask for your consent before setting non-essential analytics cookies.

  • Essential — needed for the site to work (for example remembering your cookie choice in local storage). No consent banner required for these alone.
  • Analytics (optional) — Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity measure visits, waitlist conversion, and (for Clarity) anonymised session recordings / heatmaps. These scripts load only if you choose Accept analytics.

You can choose Essential only to refuse analytics, or change your mind later via Cookie settings in the footer. A fuller policy will replace this page before broader public launch.
